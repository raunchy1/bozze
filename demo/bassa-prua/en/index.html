Bassa Prua is a holiday home on the harbour of Palau: three independent rooms, cared for in every detail.

Every room is independent, with its own bathroom, television and air conditioning included in the price. There is a shared kitchen available during your stay.

What sets us apart is the care for details, the furniture and the taste with which the rooms are decorated, and above all the kindness: we treat our guests personally, throughout their stay.

The house

Three rooms, all with their own bathroom, independent, with television and air conditioning included in the price.

Palau and the islands

The harbour, the beach and the boat trips, all on foot from the house.
